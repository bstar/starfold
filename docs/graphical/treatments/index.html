Readable at working distance.

16 px filenames, 36 px rows and stronger secondary text. Larger letters, with enough entries visible to work quickly.

Two equal panes. One clear focus.

Paths belong to pane headers. A cursor rail identifies the active side; selection, marks and transfer direction carry the hierarchy.

A persistent command strip, contextual hints and a searchable Places picker. Inspection stays compact until requested.
